NIF at checkout, a clear order button, price per kilo, delivery times, product safety and allergens for WooCommerce shops in Spain.

== Description ==

Mostrador adds the checkout, pricing, product information and tax settings that shops in Spain need: NIF/NIE/CIF field, order button wording, unit prices, delivery times, GPSR and food information. Its screens are in Spanish because its users are in Spain, and it is translated into German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch and Polish for shops run from elsewhere.

**Sell in Spain without patches.** Mostrador brings together, in one plugin, what a Spanish shop has to show and calculate and WooCommerce doesn't include: your customer's NIF, an order button that makes clear the order must be paid, the price per kilo or per litre, delivery times, product safety and allergens. A wizard gets it ready in five minutes.

= What you gain =

* Customers who ask for an invoice with their NIF, NIE or CIF already typed in and checked, with no mistakes to fix later.
* An order button with the wording the law asks for ("Pedido con obligación de pago").
* Clear prices: "IVA incluido", shipping costs and the price per unit next to the price.
* Your conditions and the withdrawal form in the order email, without touching templates.
* Manufacturer, warnings (GPSR) and the 14 allergens in a product tab, and a "Sin gluten" or "Sin leche" filter for your customers.
* Spain's IVA rates created for you, with Canarias, Ceuta and Melilla without IVA.
* A shop check that tells you what's missing and takes you to fix it.

= Everything in the free version =

* NIF, NIE and CIF field at checkout, checked as the customer types: optional, required, or required for companies. Works with the classic checkout and the block checkout.
* Order button text: "Pedido con obligación de pago", "Comprar y pagar", "Confirmar y pagar" or your own.
* Price per unit (per kilo, litre, metre…) from the product's weight or your own quantity.
* "IVA incluido" and shipping notes next to the price, on product pages and lists.
* Delivery time ("plazo de entrega") per product, per variation or for the whole shop, with a separate time for backorders.
* Product safety (GPSR): manufacturer, EU responsible person, warnings and an image, in a product tab.
* Food information: ingredients with allergens in bold, the 14 allergens ("alérgenos"), nutrition table, with an allergen filter widget and block.
* Your terms, withdrawal information and form (Annex B) in the first order email.
* Digital content consent at checkout, kept with the order.
* IVA rates (21 %, 10 %, 4 % and 0 %), prices with IVA included, and zero IVA for Canarias, Ceuta and Melilla.
* Two styles for the product information, matched to your theme.
* A shop check with what's ready and what to review.

= Mostrador Pro =

For shops that sell to other businesses or have many products: automatic recargo de equivalencia for retailer customers, IRPF withholding on sales to companies and professionals, EU VAT number checks to sell without IVA to EU businesses, bulk editing and CSV import, a quarterly summary for your accountant, product metrics and six styles with your colours. 49 € a year for one shop.

= Important =

Mostrador is a tool: it is not legal or tax advice, and each shop remains responsible for its texts and its taxes. Mostrador issues no invoices, numbers nothing and keeps no invoice records, so keep using your invoicing program or plugin; it will show the NIF and taxes of the order.

= Your data =

The NIF is stored only in your shop, with the order and the customer's account, and it can be exported and erased with the WordPress privacy tools. The free version connects to no outside service.

== Installation ==

1. Install and activate WooCommerce.
2. Install Mostrador from Plugins > Add New, or upload the zip, and activate it.
3. The setup wizard opens. Choose where your shop is, your taxes, your checkout, your products and your emails. You can skip any step and change it later in Mostrador > Settings.
4. Open Mostrador > Shop check to see what's still missing.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does it create invoices? =

No. Mostrador doesn't create or number invoices. Your invoicing plugin will show the order's NIF and taxes.

= Does it work with the block checkout? =

Yes, with the classic checkout and the block checkout.

= What if I already use WC APG NIF? =

Mostrador detects it and leaves your field as it is.

= Does it work in the Canary Islands? =

Yes, except for the IGIC rates, which it doesn't create. The same goes for IPSI in Ceuta and Melilla.

= Will it change the tax rates I already have? =

No. If you already have rates for Spain, Mostrador only offers to add the ones that are missing. It never edits or deletes yours.

= Does it work with my theme? =

It is tested with Storefront, Astra, GeneratePress, Kadence, OceanWP, Hello Elementor and Twenty Twenty-Five, on phones and computers.

= What happens if I uninstall it? =

Your settings stay unless you tick "Delete all Mostrador data when uninstalling". The NIF and other details saved with orders and products always stay, because your invoices and records may rely on them.

= How much is Pro? =

49 € a year for one shop, or 99 € a year for up to 10 shops. Taxes extra where they apply. You can ask for a refund within 14 days.
