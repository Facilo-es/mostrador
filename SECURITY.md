# Security

Please report security problems to support@facilo.es. We answer within 2 working days.

## Scope

Mostrador adds a tax ID (NIF/NIE/CIF) field to the WooCommerce checkout, sets up Spanish IVA rates, and shows product information (price per unit, delivery time, product safety, food and allergen information) and your conditions in the order email. Reports about any of that are in scope.

Mostrador issues no invoices, numbers nothing, keeps no invoice records. It is not invoicing software under VERI*FACTU (Real Decreto 1007/2023) and stores no billing records of its own.

Personal data it handles: the customer's tax ID on the order and the account, and the customer's consent to receive digital content straight away. Both stay in the merchant's own WordPress database, and both can be exported and erased with the WordPress privacy tools. Mostrador makes no outside requests.

## Support period

Each Mostrador release gets security fixes for 5 years from its release date. Mostrador Pro gets security fixes for 5 years from purchase, and they reach lapsed Pro licences on request.

The software bill of materials is in `sbom.cdx.json` (CycloneDX 1.5).
